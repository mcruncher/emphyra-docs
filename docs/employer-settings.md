# Employer Settings

Add your organization's details, then define the positions, employment types and work locations you'll use across Emphyra.

Now that your account is ready, the next step is to tell Emphyra a little more about your organization.

This guide walks you through Employer Settings, the page that holds your organization's profile and the lists your team will pick from later. By the end, you'll know what each tab is for and what to fill in first.

## Open Employer Settings

Select Employer Settings in the sidebar. You'll see four tabs along the top: General, Positions, Employment Types and Work Locations.

We'll start with General, then move through the other three in order.

## Complete your general details

The General tab is your organization's profile. It's split into four cards, and each card has a pencil icon in the corner. Select it to edit just that card, so you can update one area without touching the rest.

### General Details

This is your organization's identity: the registered name, country and a short description of what you do. You'll already see the name and country you entered when you created your organization.

### Contact

Add the email address, phone number and address people should use to reach your organization.

### Statutory Info

This is where your compliance numbers live, such as provident fund, social security and tax numbers. The fields you see here depend on the country you selected during setup.

### Settings

This card controls how employee IDs are created. When automatic ID generation is turned on, Emphyra gives every new employee an ID for you, so there's nothing to type or keep track of when you add people.

:::tip
You don't need to fill in every field today. Add what you have now and come back for the rest, since you can edit any card at any time.
:::

## Define your positions

Positions are the job titles in your organization. Open the Positions tab to see the list.

Each position has a name, an optional description, and two dates: when it takes effect and when it ends. Leave the end date empty if the position has no planned end.

To add a position:

1. Select Add at the top right.
2. Enter the position name and, if you like, a short description.
3. Set the date it takes effect, then save.

Once positions are in place, you can assign them to employees as you add them.

> **Tip**
> Effective dates let you retire a position without losing its history. Set an end date instead of removing it.

## Choose your employment types

Employment types describe how someone works for you. Emphyra starts you off with a set of common ones, so most organizations can begin right away:

- Apprentice
- Intern
- Contract
- Permanent

If your organization uses another type, select Add on the Employment Types tab and create your own.

## Add your work locations

Work locations are the places your team works from, whether that's a head office, a branch or a site.

1. Open the Work Locations tab
2. Select Add for each one.

You'll be able to choose from this list whenever you add or update an employee.

> **Tip**
> Each tab shows a page of records at a time. If your list grows, use the arrows at the bottom right to move between pages.

## What's next

With your employer details in place, you're ready to start bringing your organization into Emphyra: adding employees, defining roles and configuring leave policies.
